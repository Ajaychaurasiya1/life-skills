## Question 3 : What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

Active and diffused mode are the parts of two thinking mode where in active mode the human brain are occupy with intense thinking about some certain topic in order to figure out the solution of particular problem and in diffuse mode the human brain is distracted away from the full focus
