# Listening and active communication

## 1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

The steps/strategies to do in active listening is
1. Always focus on what the speaker is saying
2. Paraphrase the conversation parts to make sure both on the same page
3. Do not distract your thoughts while someone is speaking.
4. Take notes of the important things so it won't slip from the mind if necessary
5. make sure to be in the good environment, so able to listen all conversation very clearly
6. Ask if there is something which is not comprehensible by you.


## 2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

According to Fisher's model the key points of reflective listening is 
1. Actively engaged, listener have to be actively engaged with the speaker conversation and
eliminating all the distraction including mental and physicall, so he willbe able to understand the speaker very throughly.
2. Authentic empathy, the listener have to view the conversation in the speaker perspective, so he will be able to 
understand with more clarification.
3. Mirroring the speaker mood, listener have to change his/her way so it will resemble the speaker nature.
