# Focus Management

## Question 1 : What is Deep Work?

Deep work can be defined as ability to focus with distraction on cognitively demanding task, hard focus is known phenomenon
but now we have name which is deep work

## Question 2 : According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?

He explained in three phases in the following ways.
1. schedule distraction => we have to schedule our distraction like when we are allow to have distraction.
2. Deep work Ritual => we have to create a habit of deep work on daily basis and increase the hour quantity.
3. Evening shutdown => we have to completely shutdown ourself that means we have to focus on sleep.

## Question 3 : How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?

1. Schedule Distraction Periods: Instead of checking notification in our phone again and again, we need to schedule to distract ourself.
2. Develop a Rhythmic Ritual: We have create a habit of deep work daily and increase the deep work quantity
3. Establish a Daily Shutdown Ritual: To protect our cognitive ability and sleep, we have to do evening shutdown, like planning the 
next day, or reviewing our days
